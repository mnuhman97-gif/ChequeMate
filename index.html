How reminders work on iPhone

ChequeMate saves reminder preferences and shows due/overdue items inside the app. A web app cannot reliably schedule native local notifications by itself on iOS. Use the “Add reminders to Calendar” option to export calendar events, then import them into Apple Calendar and check its alert settings.
